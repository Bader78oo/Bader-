# Voice script — lesson-03-sunken-ship

Record each line as `<key>.mp3` in this folder, then add the key to `manifest.json` (e.g. `["hello", "m1"]`). Lines not listed in the manifest fall back to the browser's speech synthesis.

Tips: one line per file, a short silence at both ends, mono 44.1 kHz, about 96 kbps MP3.
Educational lines (answers, facts, habitats) should be read in clear Fusha exactly as written;
Salem's lines are in Omani dialect — adjust wording to natural Omani speech before recording.
